Paris, France – The City of Lights & Love

Paris is more than a postcard-perfect Eiffel Tower shot. It’s café culture, riverside walks, late-night conversations, and a deep sense of art and history in every corner.

Best Time to Visit

April–June, September–October (mild weather, fewer crowds).

Ideal Budget (per day)

₹9,000 – ₹15,000 (mid-range stay, metro, museums & café food).

Trip Type

Romantic • Cultural • Food Lover

Must-Visit Highlights

  • Sunset view of the Eiffel Tower from Trocadéro.
  • Louvre Museum – home of the Mona Lisa and world art heritage.
  • Seine River cruise for a relaxed city overview.
  • Montmartre & Sacré-Cœur – bohemian vibes and city views.

Local Food to Try

  • Croissants & pain au chocolat from a local boulangerie.
  • Crêpes (sweet & savoury) from street-side stalls.
  • French onion soup, escargot, and authentic crème brûlée.

Safety & Tips

Paris is generally safe, but watch out for pickpockets, especially near tourist hotspots and in the metro. Buy a metro pass for 3–5 days if you plan to explore a lot. A few basic French phrases like bonjour (hello) and merci (thank you) go a long way in getting friendly service.

Tokyo, Japan – Neon Future Meets Ancient Tradition

Tokyo is a high-speed blend of cutting-edge technology, anime culture, historic temples, and some of the world’s most disciplined public transport systems.

Best Time to Visit

March–April (cherry blossoms) & October–November (autumn colours).

Ideal Budget (per day)

₹8,000 – ₹13,000 (depending on neighbourhood & food choices).

Trip Type

Tech • Culture • Anime • Food

Must-Visit Highlights

  • Shibuya Crossing – the world-famous “scramble” crossing.
  • Sensō-ji Temple in Asakusa – Tokyo’s oldest temple.
  • Akihabara – heaven for gamers, anime lovers & tech fans.
  • TeamLab Planets / Borderless – digital immersive art experience.

Local Food to Try

  • Ramen (try different broths – shoyu, miso, tonkotsu).
  • Sushi & sashimi from a local conveyor-belt restaurant.
  • Takoyaki, okonomiyaki, and convenience-store snacks.

Productivity Tips for Travellers

Get a Suica or Pasmo card for seamless metro travel. Use Google Maps and hyperdia-like apps for trains, and always keep cash, as some smaller shops still prefer it. Most public spaces have vending machines, so staying hydrated on the go is easy and cheap.

Bali, Indonesia – Nature, Culture & Digital-Nomad Paradise

Bali is where lush rice fields, temples, waterfalls, and beach clubs meet a huge international community of travellers and remote workers.

Best Time to Visit

April–October (dry season, great for beaches & bikes).

Ideal Budget (per day)

₹4,500 – ₹9,000 (Bali can be very affordable if planned well).

Trip Type

Nature • Spiritual • Relax • Remote Work

Must-Visit Highlights

  • Tegallalang rice terraces near Ubud.
  • Ulun Danu Beratan & Tanah Lot temples.
  • Beaches of Canggu, Seminyak and Uluwatu.
  • Waterfalls like Tegenungan & Sekumpul.

Local Food to Try

  • Nasi Goreng (fried rice) & Mie Goreng (fried noodles).
  • Satay skewers with peanut sauce.
  • Fresh smoothie bowls and local Bali coffee.

Work & Wi-Fi Tips

Bali is full of co-working cafés with strong Wi-Fi and power sockets, especially in Canggu and Ubud. If you’re planning a workation, choose accommodation that mentions reliable internet in reviews. Scooters are the easiest way to move around, but always wear a helmet.

New York City, USA – The City That Never Sleeps

New York is a fast-paced blend of skyscrapers, Broadway shows, iconic parks, and neighbourhoods that feel like different countries altogether.

Best Time to Visit

April–June & September–November (pleasant weather, lots of events).

Ideal Budget (per day)

₹10,000 – ₹18,000 (NYC can be expensive, but can be optimised).

Trip Type

Urban • Shopping • Entertainment

Must-Visit Highlights

  • Central Park – a green oasis in the concrete jungle.
  • Times Square & Broadway show experience.
  • Brooklyn Bridge walk at sunrise or sunset.
  • Statue of Liberty & Ellis Island ferry tour.

Local Food to Try

  • New York-style pizza slices.
  • Bagels with cream cheese & lox.
  • Food trucks – hotdogs, halal carts, tacos.

Money-Saving Tips

Get a MetroCard (or equivalent) for unlimited subway and bus use. Consider city passes that bundle entry to major attractions. Many museums have “pay what you wish” days – perfect for budget-conscious travellers.

Dubai, UAE – Desert Dreams & Futuristic Skylines

Dubai combines luxury malls, desert safaris, skyscrapers, beaches, and cultural quarters like Old Dubai – all within a highly connected, safe city.

Best Time to Visit

November–March (cooler weather, ideal for outdoor activities).

Ideal Budget (per day)

₹7,000 – ₹14,000 (depending on stay & attractions).

Trip Type

Luxury • Desert • Family • Shopping

Must-Visit Highlights

  • Burj Khalifa observation deck.
  • Desert safari with dune bashing & BBQ dinner.
  • Dubai Marina & JBR Beach walk.
  • Old Dubai – Gold Souk & Spice Souk.

Local Food & Culture

  • Shawarma, falafel, and hummus platters.
  • Arabic coffee and dates.
  • Local Emirati dishes like Machboos and Harees.

Dress Code & Etiquette

Dubai is modern but still rooted in tradition. Dress modestly in malls and public places, especially in Old Dubai. Beachwear is fine at pools and private beaches. Public transport is clean, safe, and highly efficient.
